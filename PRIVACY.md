## MineColor Privacy Policy

This policy covers the current MineColor test version, including its closed test on Google Play. This version does not enable in-app accounts, cloud progress backup, advertising, in-app purchases or paid offline access. Coloring requires an internet connection.

### 1. Information kept on your device

Your coloring progress, completed pictures, favorites, albums, hint usage, achievements, coloring statistics and preferences are saved locally on your device. Our coloring service does not receive your filled regions, coloring sequence, saved picture files or these local records. This version does not provide cross-device progress synchronization.

The artwork collection is included with the app installation. Including artwork on your device does not make coloring an offline feature: the app connects to our service for short-lived coloring access.

### 2. Information processed by the online service

When you connect, our service necessarily receives your IP address. To establish a temporary session and authorize access to a picture, the app also sends a random session request value and the resource identifier or cryptographic digest of the picture being opened. Requests carry temporary authorization tokens. The service processes request results and limited operational information to deliver the service, control excessive requests, protect artwork and diagnose reliability issues.

These requests do not require your name, email address, date of birth, Google password, contacts, precise location or advertising identifier. Session and authorization records are temporary technical records, not a player account or an advertising profile. A short-lived coloring authorization lasts no more than 30 seconds and is renewed while connected; it does not grant indefinite offline access.

### 3. Saving pictures and sharing

Saving a picture writes the image you selected to your device using the operating system's media facilities. Sharing opens the system sharing interface and passes the selected image to an app or recipient you choose. MineColor does not automatically upload your pictures to our server, create a public sharing link or send an email in this test version. The receiving app or service handles anything you send under its own privacy policy.

These features do not require access to your contacts, microphone, precise location or entire photo library. You remain in control of whether to save or share an image.

### 4. Contacting us

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to provide. We use them to respond to your request, investigate reported problems, consider suggestions and handle privacy inquiries. Please send only information needed for your request. Contacting support does not automatically attach your artwork, local progress or account credentials.

### 5. Service providers and international processing

- **Amazon Web Services:** our coloring access and content service is hosted on AWS Lightsail in Singapore. AWS processes connection and infrastructure information needed to operate and secure the service. See [AWS data protection](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/data-protection/).

- **Google Play:** Google distributes the Android test app, manages test enrollment and may process store, installation and diagnostic information under its own terms and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Joining a Google Play test does not create a MineColor account.

- **Email:** messages you send to our Gmail contact address are handled by Google's email service as well as by us.

- **This policy website:** this public policy page is hosted by GitHub. GitHub may process IP addresses, browser and request information, and security logs to deliver and protect the page, subject to its [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). We have not added advertising, analytics scripts, external fonts or data-entry forms to this page. Reading this policy does not require a GitHub account or login.

If you access our coloring service from outside Singapore, the connection information described above is processed in Singapore. Hosting and email providers may process information in other countries under their own policies. We do not sell player personal information or use our coloring service to create targeted advertising profiles. We disclose information where required by applicable law or as necessary to protect the security of our service.

### 6. Retention and deletion

- **Local records:** remain on your device until you delete them, clear app data or uninstall the app. This removes progress, settings and other local app records and can be irreversible. Images exported to your photo library, another app or a recipient must be deleted separately. Operating-system backup and destination-app behavior are controlled by those providers.

- **Temporary service records:** session, authorization and request-limiting records expire and are removed from process memory through bounded cleanup; they are not stored as a cloud player profile. Operational monitoring uses bounded, in-memory aggregates rather than a history of individual painting actions.

- **Support messages:** are retained as needed to respond to the request and meet applicable legal or security obligations. You can ask us to delete correspondence associated with your email address. Any information that must be retained for a legitimate legal or security reason is limited to that purpose.

There is no MineColor cloud account or server-side coloring progress to delete in this version. To request access, correction or deletion of personal information you have provided to us, or to raise a privacy concern, email [edwardsocool@gmail.com](mailto:edwardsocool@gmail.com). We will handle requests as required by applicable law and may ask for reasonable confirmation that the request concerns your information.

### 7. Security

The app uses HTTPS to connect to our public service. Access to server systems is restricted, and content storage and backups use encryption at rest. Local data is protected by your device and operating system; keep your device secure. No online service or storage method can guarantee absolute security.

### 8. Children and parents

MineColor is designed for people aged 8 and above, including children and adults. Parents and guardians should supervise children's use as appropriate. The current test version does not display advertisements, offer in-app purchases or provide player accounts, public profiles or in-app chat. It does not ask a child to enter their name, date of birth, email address, school, phone number or precise location.

The limited technical information described in section 2 is used to provide and protect the coloring service, not to build a child's advertising profile or track them for behavioral advertising. A child should ask a parent or guardian to contact support or send feedback on their behalf. Please do not include a child's full name, address, school, account credentials or other unnecessary personal information in a message or attachment.

A parent or guardian can contact [edwardsocool@gmail.com](mailto:edwardsocool@gmail.com) to ask about information provided by their child or to request access, correction or deletion. If we learn that personal information has been provided contrary to applicable children's privacy requirements, we will take appropriate steps, including deletion where required. Requirements for parental consent and children's privacy vary by country or region; planned features that require additional age or consent handling are not enabled by this policy.

### 9. Changes to this policy

We will update this policy before enabling features that change how personal information is handled, such as advertising, purchases, cloud accounts or public media uploads. The date at the top identifies this version. This policy describes the current test app and does not imply that planned features are already available.

## MineColor 隐私政策

适用应用：MineColor: Color by Number；Android包名：`com.colorforyou.coloring`。运营者：wangjiawei。隐私联系邮箱：[edwardsocool@gmail.com](mailto:edwardsocool@gmail.com)。

本政策适用于当前MineColor测试版本，包括Google Play封闭测试。本次版本不启用应用账号、云进度备份、广告、应用内购买或付费离线服务。实际填色需要联网。

### 一、保存在设备上的信息

填色进度、完成的画作、收藏、相册、提示使用记录、成就、填色统计和设置保存在你的设备本地。我们的填色服务不接收已填区域、填色顺序、作品图片文件或这些本机记录。本版本不提供跨设备进度同步。

图库随应用安装，但画作内置不代表支持离线填色：应用仍需要连接服务器取得短时填色许可。

### 二、联网服务处理的信息

设备连接服务时，服务器必然会接收到连接IP地址。为建立临时会话并允许打开画布，应用还会发送随机会话请求值，以及当前画布的资源标识或摘要；后续请求带有临时授权令牌。服务会处理请求结果和有限的运行信息，用于提供服务、限制过量请求、保护画布资源和排查稳定性问题。

上述请求不要求你的姓名、邮箱、出生日期、Google密码、通讯录、精确位置或广告标识符。临时会话与授权记录不是玩家账号或广告画像。短时填色许可最长30秒，在联网时续期，不提供无限期离线使用权限。

### 三、保存图片和分享

保存图片会通过系统媒体接口，将你选择的作品写入设备。分享会打开系统分享界面，只把所选图片交给你主动选择的应用或接收方。本次测试版不会自动上传作品到我们的服务器、生成公开分享链接或发送邮件。接收图片的其他应用或服务按各自的隐私政策处理你发送的内容。

这些功能不需要访问通讯录、麦克风、精确位置或整套照片库。是否保存或分享图片由你决定。

### 四、邮件联系和反馈

当你主动发邮件给我们时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、邮件内容，以及你自行添加的附件，用于回复请求、排查问题、了解建议和处理隐私咨询。请仅提供处理问题所必需的信息。联系支持不会自动附上作品、完整存档或账号凭据。

- **Amazon Web Services：**填色许可与内容服务部署在AWS Lightsail新加坡区域，AWS会处理运行和保护基础设施所需的连接及技术信息。参见[AWS数据保护说明](https://aws.amazon.com/compliance/data-protection/)。

- **Google Play：**Google负责Android测试应用的分发和测试加入资格，并可能按其条款及[Google隐私政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy)处理商店、安装和诊断信息。加入Google Play测试不等于注册MineColor账号。

- **邮件服务：**你发送到本政策Gmail联系地址的邮件由Google邮件服务及我们处理。

- **本政策网页：**本公开政策页面由GitHub托管。GitHub可能为传输和保护网页处理IP地址、浏览器和请求信息及安全日志，具体遵循[GitHub隐私声明](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)。我们未在本页添加广告、分析脚本、外部字体或收集信息的表单。阅读本政策不需要GitHub账号或登录。

如果你从新加坡以外地区使用填色服务，上述必要连接信息会在新加坡处理。托管及邮件提供商也可能按其隐私规则在其他国家或地区处理信息。我们不出售玩家个人信息，也不利用填色服务建立定向广告画像。依法需要披露，或为保护服务安全必须披露时，我们会按适用法律处理。

### 六、保存期限、删除与隐私请求

- **本机记录：**保留到你主动删除、清除应用数据或卸载应用。该操作会删除本机进度、设置等记录，可能无法恢复。已导出到相册、其他应用或接收方的图片需在相应位置另行删除。系统备份与目标应用的处理方式由相应提供商控制。

- **临时服务记录：**会话、许可和限流记录在短时有效期到期后，通过有界清理从进程内存移除，不保存为云端玩家资料。运行监控仅使用有界、进程内的汇总，不保存逐次填色操作历史。

- **支持邮件：**为处理请求及满足适用法律、安全义务所需的时间保留。你可以要求删除与你的邮箱相关的联系记录；依法或出于正当安全原因必须保留的部分仅用于相应用途。

本版本没有可删除的MineColor云账号或服务器端画作进度。如果你希望访问、更正或删除自己向我们提供的个人信息，或提出隐私问题，请联系[edwardsocool@gmail.com](mailto:edwardsocool@gmail.com)。我们会按适用法律处理，并可能要求适度确认请求确实涉及你的信息。
